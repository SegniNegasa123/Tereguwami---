# TEREGUWAMI (ተርጓሚ) — Institutional Grant & Academic Submission Proposal (Document B)

**Project Title**: Tereguwami: An Adaptive Multimodal AI System for Two-Way Communication Between Ethiopian Sign Language Users and Non-Signers  
**Principal Investigator**: Segni Seyoum Negasa  
**Host Institution**: EGATE (School of Gifted and Talented)  
**Academic Collaborators**: Addis Ababa University AI & Computational Linguistics Faculty  
**Target Sector**: Accessibility Infrastructure / Digital Public Goods / Healthcare & Judicial Access  

---

## 1. Project Abstract
Communication barriers between Deaf and hearing Ethiopians create severe systemic exclusion across healthcare, justice, banking, and public education. Tereguwami addresses this structural challenge by engineering the first open, continuous, bidirectional Ethiopian Sign Language (ESL / ETHSL) translation system. The architecture couples computer vision perception (543 3D landmarks via MediaPipe Holistic), early-fusion non-manual grammatical modeling, gloss-free sequence-to-sequence transformers with Afrocentric decoders (Amharic, Afaan Oromo, English), a generative 3D avatar reverse channel, and a non-invasive neuromotor silent-speech interface. Evaluated on a newly curated 60–100 signer-independent benchmark dataset, the project overcomes the documented 21-percentage-point generalization collapse of prior isolated models and provides an operational blueprint for UNCRPD accessibility compliance.

## 2. Institutional Significance & Alignment
1. **UNCRPD Compliance**: Ethiopia ratified the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities, legally binding public entities to provide accessible information channels.
2. **First East African Benchmark**: While PHOENIX-2014T (German), How2Sign (American), and CSL-Daily (Chinese) drive international NLP research, no open benchmark exists for Ethiopian Sign Language. Tereguwami creates this foundational infrastructure.
3. **Deaf Community Co-Creation**: Guided by a standing Deaf-Led Advisory Board holding binding approval and veto authority.

## 3. Phased Milestones
- **Month 1–3**: Perception pipeline & initial 15-signer pilot data collection under board oversight.
- **Month 4–6**: Isolated recognition baseline reproducing 2025 SOTA, followed by gloss-free transformer pretraining.
- **Month 7–9**: Early-fusion facial semantics integration and generative 3D avatar rendering engine.
- **Month 10–12**: Edge optimization (ONNX), institutional broadcaster / court pilots, and public benchmark leaderboard release.
