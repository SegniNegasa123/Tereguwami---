# Ethiopian Sign Language Benchmark: Participant Informed Consent Agreement (§10.3, §16)

**Project Title**: Tereguwami (ተርጓሚ) — Open Ethiopian Sign Language Continuous Benchmark Dataset  
**Principal Investigator**: Segni Seyoum Negasa  
**Host Institution**: EGATE (School of Gifted and Talented) in partnership with Ethiopian Deaf Community Organizations  
**Oversight Body**: Standing Deaf-Led Advisory Board  

---

## 1. Purpose of the Study
You are invited to contribute to the creation of the first open, continuous, signer-independent benchmark dataset for Ethiopian Sign Language (ESL / ETHSL). The objective is to enable automated two-way translation between Deaf signers and hearing non-signers across healthcare, legal, educational, and civic settings.

## 2. Participant Rights & Video Footage Protections
1. **Voluntary Participation**: Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may pause, rest, or conclude recording sessions at any point without penalty.
2. **Access Control**: Raw video footage is stored in access-restricted, encrypted storage tiers. Raw video is NEVER committed to public software version control repositories.
3. **Unconditional Withdrawal Right**: You retain the permanent and enforceable right to request the removal and deletion of your video footage and associated keypoints from all future benchmark releases at any time.
4. **Compensation**: Signers are compensated at fair, above-market rates determined in consultation with the Deaf-Led Advisory Board for their time and linguistic expertise.

## 3. Deaf Community Oversight
All recorded footage and linguistic annotations are reviewed by the Deaf-Led Advisory Board prior to any release to verify cultural and grammatical accuracy and prevent derogatory representations.
