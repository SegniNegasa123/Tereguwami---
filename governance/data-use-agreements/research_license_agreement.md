# Tereguwami Research Data Use Agreement (DUA) (§10.3)

**Dataset**: Ethiopian Sign Language (ESL / ETHSL) Continuous Multi-Modal Benchmark  
**License**: Non-Commercial Research & Educational Use License  

---

## Terms & Conditions
1. **Academic Non-Commercial Scope**: The dataset is provided solely for academic research, linguistic inquiry, and non-commercial open-source accessibility development.
2. **De-Anonymization Prohibition**: Researchers shall not attempt to identify, re-identify, or track individual contributing signers beyond provided metadata identifiers.
3. **No Redistribution of Raw Videos**: Downloaded raw videos may not be mirrored, scraped, or republished without written authorization from the Deaf-Led Advisory Board.
4. **Enforcement of Participant Withdrawal**: Licensees agree to pull and remove revoked signer data upon receiving notification of signer withdrawal from the central registry.
5. **Mandatory Attribution**: Publications utilizing this dataset must cite the original benchmark paper and acknowledge the Ethiopian Deaf community contributors.
