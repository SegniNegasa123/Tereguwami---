# Deaf-Led Advisory Board Charter & Veto Protocol (§10.3, §16)

## 1. Mandate and Authority
The **Deaf-Led Advisory Board** is a permanent governing body holding decisive governance authority over the Tereguwami research program, datasets, and public deployments.
The board exists to ensure that Ethiopian Sign Language technology is developed **by, with, and for** the Ethiopian Deaf community, preventing extractive research practices.

## 2. Composition
The Board consists of 5 to 7 voting members:
- 2 Representatives from the Ethiopian National Association of the Deaf (ENAD)
- 2 Certified Deaf educators or ESL linguistic specialists
- 1 Deaf youth / student advocate
- 1 Legal accessibility counsel
- 1 Research liaison (non-voting or advisory)

## 3. Formal Veto Power
The Advisory Board holds absolute and binding veto power over:
1. **Dataset Releases**: No video, landmark, or annotation benchmark can be publicly released without majority board sign-off.
2. **Annotation Verification**: Linguistic accuracy, dialectal acceptance, and cultural appropriateness must be certified by the board.
3. **Public Capability Claims**: The board reviews all public communication to prevent exaggerated claims (e.g. prohibiting "mind-reading" or claiming 100% human interpreter replacement).
4. **Institutional Deployments**: Deployments in high-stakes environments (police stations, courts, hospitals) must adhere to board-mandated human-in-the-loop safeguards.
